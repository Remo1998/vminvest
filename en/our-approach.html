We do not believe in simply distributing investment memoranda to a large database of investors.

Our approach is personal and selective.

We first seek to understand:

Who is the investor? What are they looking for? What risk-return profile is appropriate? Which markets are relevant? And which partners can genuinely add value to the opportunity?

Only then do we make targeted introductions.

Our relationships are one of our most important assets. Trust, integrity, discretion, respect for each other's relationships and a long-term perspective therefore form the foundation of every collaboration.
