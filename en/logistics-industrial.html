Logistics & Industrial

Connected locations for logistics and industry. With a focus on accessibility, power and long-term value.

VMINVEST is active in large-scale logistics and industrial real estate, focusing on both existing investment opportunities and substantial development sites and strategic land positions.

Driven by the continued growth of e-commerce, international distribution and the evolution of European supply chains, demand remains strong for well-located logistics assets with excellent access to motorways, ports, rail networks and other critical infrastructure.

Through our international network, we have access to landowners, developers, investors and occupiers and are involved with locations offering opportunities for large-scale logistics and industrial development.

Our focus includes:

  • large-scale logistics development sites;
  • distribution centres and logistics hubs;
  • strategic land positions;
  • light industrial and business space;
  • build-to-suit developments for occupiers;
  • sale-and-leaseback opportunities;
  • investment and development joint ventures;
  • locations benefiting from multimodal connectivity and strategic infrastructure.

Our assessment extends beyond the land or real estate itself. Accessibility, zoning, infrastructure, power availability, sustainability and future expansion potential are all important considerations.

Through our relationships with landowners and developers as well as investors and occupiers, VMINVEST can connect the relevant parties at an early stage and contribute to the creation of investable and future-proof logistics developments.

Location quality, sustainability and long-term value are central to our approach within logistics real estate.
