Growth Finance & Growth Capital

We connect real estate developers and entrepreneurs with appropriate development finance and growth capital partners.

ExploreReal Estate & InvestmentsData Centers & Data InfrastructureLogistics & IndustrialGrowth Finance & Growth CapitalSustainability & EnergyHotels & HospitalityResidential & Senior LivingOff-Market OpportunitiesInternational NetworkOur Approach

Alongside our real estate and development activities, VMINVEST has access to an international network of capital providers supporting growth and development.

We work with a number of capital providers offering growth and development financing for real estate projects, both in the Netherlands and internationally. This can cover a broad range of real estate sectors and different stages of development, enabling us to connect developers and property owners with appropriate sources of capital.

In addition, we maintain relationships with promising companies seeking growth capital, as well as investors, family offices, private equity firms and other capital providers actively looking for businesses and investment opportunities with attractive growth potential.

Our activities in this area include:

  • growth and development financing for real estate;
  • financing across a broad range of real estate developments;
  • growth capital for companies;
  • private equity and other forms of equity capital;
  • family offices and private investors;
  • connecting entrepreneurs and developers with appropriate capital providers;
  • international investment and financing opportunities.

Here too, VMINVEST's strength lies in connecting the right parties. Through relationships on both the demand and supply side of capital, we can make targeted introductions between real estate developers, entrepreneurs, investors and financing partners.

Our focus is on businesses and projects with a clear strategy, compelling growth potential and a solid foundation for further development.
