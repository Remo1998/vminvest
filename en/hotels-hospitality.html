Hotels and hospitality have been an important part of VMINVEST's activities for many years.

We maintain relationships with hotel owners, international operators, developers, institutional investors, family offices and private-equity investors.

Our activities include existing hotels, hotel developments, serviced apartments and other extended-stay hospitality concepts.

Through our relationships with both property owners and operators, we can not only identify investment opportunities but also connect parties in acquisitions, sale-and-leaseback structures, long-term lease transactions and new developments.

VMINVEST is particularly active across a number of European markets.
