Data Centers & Data Infrastructure

Strategic locations, power capacity and international capital come together in the infrastructure of tomorrow.

VMINVEST has significantly expanded its activities in data centers and data infrastructure.

The global expansion of cloud computing, digitalisation and artificial intelligence is driving unprecedented demand for suitable data center locations, power capacity and sustainable infrastructure.

Within this market, VMINVEST has developed direct relationships with landowners, developers, investors and specialised partners active in the international data center sector.

This allows us to operate on both sides of the market.

We work with landowners and developers controlling sites that are suitable, or potentially suitable, for data center development, while simultaneously maintaining relationships with investors and capital providers seeking exposure to this rapidly growing sector.

Our focus includes:

  • strategic land positions;
  • greenfield data center developments;
  • existing and future power capacity;
  • large-scale data center campuses;
  • equity and joint-venture structures;
  • partnerships between landowners and developers;
  • international investors and infrastructure capital;
  • sustainable energy and infrastructure solutions.

VMINVEST can therefore act as a bridge between landowners, developers, capital and end users.
